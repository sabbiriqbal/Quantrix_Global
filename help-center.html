1. Getting Started

Create your account

  1. 1. Install the app from the app store and open it. The intro screen explains what the app does.
  2. 2. Choose Sign Up and fill in: Full Name, Username, Email, Mobile Number and a password.
  3. 3. A 6-digit OTP code is emailed to you. Enter it to verify your email.
  4. 4. Open the verification email or reset link sent to you if prompted.

Complete your profile

  1. 1. After verification you'll be asked to complete your profile (optional): mobile number, residential/business address and a profile photo.
  2. 2. Tap Continue when done — you can skip any item and add it later.

Initial security setup

  1. 1. Set up your App Lock: create a PIN and/or enable fingerprint / Face ID unlock.
  2. 2. Optionally add a second layer with an authenticator app (TOTP) by scanning the QR code shown in setup.
  3. 3. Save the emergency recovery passkeys shown at the end of setup — keep them somewhere safe outside the device.

Remember: if you lock yourself out, PIN and password cannot be recovered by anyone — that's by design. Recovery passkeys are the only way back in, so store them safely.

2. First Look & Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home. It shows an overview of everything at a glance:

  • Balance card — total balance across your enabled accounts, with income vs. expense statistics.
  • Wallet accounts — stacked cards of your accounts.
  • Recent transactions — the latest ledger entries.
  • Loan quick view — amounts you lent out or borrowed.
  • Project / entity overview - totals due for your projects and stakeholders.
  • Category breakdown & budgets — where your money goes and progress on limits.
  • Recurring calendar — upcoming scheduled payments.

Use the floating grid menu (or the bottom bar) to reach every feature. A guided tour highlights each part of the screen the first time you open the dashboard — you can replay it anytime from settings.

3. Accounts & Wallets

Create your first account

  1. 1. Open Accounts (Account Manager) from the menu.
  2. 2. Tap + Add and pick a type: Bank, Cash, Mobile Financial Services (mfs), Savings, Investments or Others.
  3. 3. Enter a name, the account number (optional — only stored for your records), the initial balance and currency.
  4. 4. Personalise the card: choose a colour and icon, then save.
  5. 5. Disable an account's toggle if you want it excluded from the total balance.

Accounts never connect to your bank — balances represent only what you enter or record. Everything syncs back to the ledger automatically when you record transactions.

4. Recording Transactions

Add an income, expense or transfer

  1. 1. Tap the + Add button (usually in the transactions tab or dashboard).
  2. 2. Pick the type: Credit (income), Debit (expense) or Transfer (between your own accounts).
  3. 3. Enter the amount. Tip: open the built-in calculator for quick math with the result filled in.
  4. 4. Choose the source account (and destination account for transfers).
  5. 5. Assign a category (e.g. Food, Transport) — create your own custom categories from the category manager.
  6. 6. Optionally add a stakeholder (who it's for), a project/cost head, tags, and a description or note.
  7. 7. Optionally attach a receipt photo — see the next section for scanning.
  8. 8. Save. The account balance and all dashboard statistics update instantly.

Editing and organising

  • Open the Ledger to see all records, grouped by month, with search.
  • Use custom filters (date range, account, category, type, amount range, tags) to build a precise list.
  • Deleting a transfer deletes both legs together safely.

5. Receipt Scanning (OCR)

  1. 1. In the new transaction screen, tap Scan Receipt.
  2. 2. Take a photo of the receipt or pick one from your gallery.
  3. 3. The app reads the receipt on your device and fills in merchant, date, line items, subtotal, VAT/tax, grand total and suggests a category and payment method (e.g. cash, card, mobile financial services).
  4. 4. Check the details, adjust anything, then save the transaction.

Optional: AI-powered scanning

  1. 1. Go to settings and paste your own Gemini API key (free from Google AI Studio).
  2. 2. Now receipt photos can be parsed with AI for a more precise reading.

Privacy: standard scanning runs fully on-device and sends nothing anywhere. AI scanning sends the receipt image to Google's AI service — only if you enable it yourself.

6. Loans & Loan Payments

Use the loans screen to record and manage loan information — money you lent out or money you borrowed.

Create a loan

  1. 1. Open Loans and tap + New Loan.
  2. 2. Choose who: a person/entity name, or a saved stakeholder.
  3. 3. Choose the direction: I lent or Borrowed.
  4. 4. Enter the principal amount, date, interest rate, interest amount, duration in months, settlement date, and the source account.
  5. 5. Save. The current balance is calculated automatically and appears on the dashboard loan view.

Record payments

  1. 1. Open the loan's details and tap Add Payment.
  2. 2. Enter the amount, date and note. Payments you receive (on lent money) reduce what's owed to you; payments you make (on borrowed money) reduce what you owe.
  3. 3. Mark the loan settled when everything is repaid.

Handy tools

  • Interest calculator — model interest for simple/compound scenarios before recording anything.
  • Deleting a loan requires a confirmation and removes its payment history with it.

Loans here are information records — the app holds money nowhere and gives no financial advice.

7. Recurring Payments

  1. 1. Open Recurring and tap + New.
  2. 2. Set the type (income/expense/transfer), amount and the accounts involved.
  3. 3. Pick a frequency: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly — or custom days and pick exact dates.
  4. 4. Set start/end dates and choose:
    • Auto-execute — create the transaction automatically when due,
    • Notify — remind you with a notification instead.
  5. 5. Save. Pause or edit any rule anytime; due items appear in a dashboard calendar card.

8. Budgets & Cost Heads

Set a budget

  1. 1. Open Budgets and tap + New.
  2. 2. Choose a category, name the budget, set a spending limit, and set a start and end date.
  3. 3. Save. Progress bars on the dashboard show how spent amount tracks against each limit.

Cost heads

Create cost heads (project or department tags) from the menu, then assign them to transactions to keep group spending separate — useful for a business trip, a home renovation or any sub-project. Project ledgers show totals per cost head.

9. Stakeholders & Projects

Add a stakeholder (person or entity)

  1. 1. Open Entities (stakeholders) and tap + Add.
  2. 2. Enter a name and any of: phone, email, address, description and a photo.
  3. 3. Save. Assign this stakeholder to transactions, loans or projects; the app totals their amount due automatically and shows an entity ledger per person.

Projects

Create a project, optionally link a stakeholder and cost heads, and record project-related transactions twice through: project ledger for the group view, main ledger for the books.

10. Notes & Tasks

  1. 1. Open Notes and tap + New Note.
  2. 2. Write a title and content; use checklist items for to-dos with completion checkboxes.
  3. 3. Set a reminder date-time if you want a notification; choose repeat options (never, daily, weekly, custom days…).
  4. 4. Colour-code, pin important notes, and archive finished ones.

Optional AI assist

If you've set your own Gemini API key (Section 5), you can let AI structure quick voice-of-thought notes into clean tasks or summarise long notes — sent to the AI service only when you ask for it.

11. Statistics & Filters

  • Open the Statistics tab for charts: category breakdown, income vs. expense trends, and monthly comparisons.
  • Dashboard cards summarise balance, statement balance, credit/debit statistics, loan overview, project overview and budget progress.
  • In the ledger, combine custom filters to answer specific questions — e.g. "cash expenses above 1000 in the last 60 days".
  • Search recent entries fast with the search bar; recent searches are remembered locally for convenience.

12. Import & Export

Export your data

  1. 1. Open Settings → Backup & Restore.
  2. 2. Choose an export format: CSV, a multi-sheet Excel file (Ledger, Loans, Projects, Accounts, Categories, Budgets, Notes…), or a full JSON backup. A raw database file export is also available.
  3. 3. Save it to your device or share it via the system share sheet (e.g. email it to yourself).

Import / restore

  1. 1. Pick a backup file (CSV / Excel / JSON / database) from the same screen.
  2. 2. Review the import preview — it shows exactly which sheets, categories and records will be created.
  3. 3. Confirm. You can also download a sample import template to prepare data from other apps.

Make regular exports — your records live on your device, and exports are your portable safety net.

13. Cloud Sync & Google Drive Backup

Google Drive backup (Premium & Cloud plans)

  1. 1. Open Settings → Cloud Backup and connect your Google account.
  2. 2. Choose a schedule: Daily, Weekly or Monthly — or run a manual backup.
  3. 3. The backup goes to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive (database, full export, plus receipt/entity photos). Only this app can read it.
  4. 4. To move to a new phone, install the app, sign in, and choose Restore from the same screen.
  5. 5. To delete those backup files, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage Apps → the app → Delete hidden app data.

Cloud sync (Cloud plan)

  1. 1. With an active Cloud subscription (or trial), cloud sync is available and updates your records across your own devices.
  2. 2. You can switch the cloud database on or off in settings at any time — the app stays fully usable offline either way.

14. Security, Lock & 2FA

  • App Lock: require your PIN or biometrics (fingerprint/Face ID) when opening the app; auto-lock engages after inactivity.
  • Change PIN: settings → security. The PIN is stored only as a hash — nobody can read it, including us.
  • Biometrics: handled entirely by your device's operating system. Use your device credentials as fallback when biometrics fail.
  • Two-factor authentication (TOTP): link an authenticator app (Google Authenticator etc.) to your account. On a new device you'll need the rotating 6-digit code alongside your password.
  • Emergency recovery passkeys: long codes generated during initial setup. Each is one-time use; regenerate anytime from the security screen but note old ones become invalid.
  • Password reset: "Forgot password" sends a one-time code to your verified email, then lets you set a new password.

15. Reminders & Notifications

  • Notifications are generated locally on your device — due payments, upcoming items, note reminders, budget alerts and loan due dates. We run no push-notification service and send nothing to your device from our servers.
  • If you see no notifications, check that notifications permission is granted for the app in system settings, and that battery optimisation isn't killing it.
  • The notification log (bell icon) keeps a history of recent alerts and reminders in-app.
  • You can silence or reshelve each alert type from settings without losing the underlying records.

17. Profile & Account Settings

  • Update profile: edit your name, mobile number, address or photo from the profile screen (device photo picks never upload until you choose to).
  • Username: reserved at sign-up and shown in your profile; it's how friends could find you if you ever share records.
  • Change password: settings → account → change password (confirm with your current one).
  • Language & currency: choose from settings; the interface supports many languages with full English and Bengali dictionaries; per-item translations happen on-device.
  • Theme, text size & icons: light/dark mode, dynamic text scaling and an optional custom app icon.
  • Delete account: settings → account → Delete Account. A one-time code is emailed to confirm. This removes local data on the device, your cloud records and your sign-in account. Google Drive backups must be removed manually via Google Drive settings.

Frequently asked questions

  • Does the app hold or move my money? No — it's a records tool. All holdings stay in your real accounts.
  • Is my financial data private? Yes. It lives in a local database on your device; nothing is uploaded unless you enable optional backup or a Cloud plan.
  • Does it show ads? Never. No ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • What if I lose my phone? Your export/Drive backup restores data; without a backup, device-local records can't be recovered by anyone.
  • Can I use multiple devices? Yes — with a Cloud plan, sign in on each device to keep them in step.
