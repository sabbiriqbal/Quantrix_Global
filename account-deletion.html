Account & Data Deletion

We provide full transparency and control over your personal information. If you wish to permanently delete your account and associated data from our systems, follow the steps below.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email from your registered email address to our support team with the following details:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Body: Your registered email/phone number and registered user name.
Send Deletion Email

What Happens Next?

  • Verification: Our support team will process your request and verify your identity within 48 hours.
  • Permanent Deletion: Your profile, contact details, authentication credentials, and activity logs will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

Data Retention

Financial transactions, invoicing, or security logs may be retained for the minimum period required by applicable legal, tax, or fraud-prevention regulations.
